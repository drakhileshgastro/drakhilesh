## Conditions Treated
- **Fatty Liver:** Reversal guides for NAFLD and advanced NASH.
- **Jaundice (Pilia):** Diagnosis of hepatic and obstructive jaundice.
- **Liver Cirrhosis:** Advanced management of portal hypertension and ascites.
- **Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS):** Chronic bloating, bowel changes, and gut therapy.
- **Acid Reflux & GERD:** Heartburn management and OGD Scopy evaluation.
- **Peptic Ulcers:** Treatment of stomach ulcers and H. pylori bacteria.
- **Gallstones (Pittashay Pathri):** Biliary stone removal and clinical checkups.
- **Pancreatitis:** Acute and chronic pancreatic inflammation therapies.
- **Ulcerative Colitis:** Chronic IBD and bowel management.
- **GI Bleeding:** Emergency endoscopic bleed control.

## Diagnostic & Therapeutic Procedures
- **Upper GI Endoscopy (OGD Scopy):** 15-minute painless inspection of food pipe and stomach under light sedation.
- **Colonoscopy:** Deep large intestine inspection for polyps, colitis, or early cancers.
- **ERCP:** Biliary stone extraction, jaundice relief, and pancreatic duct stenting.
- **EUS (Endoscopic Ultrasound):** High-definition visual ultrasound from inside the tract to diagnose tumors.
- **EUS-Guided FNA & FNB:** Endoscopic needle biopsy of gastrointestinal lesions.
- **Variceal Glue Injection:** Emergency control of internal bleeding varices.
- **Polypectomy:** Preventive painless removal of precancerous polyps.
- **Balloon Dilatation:** Non-surgical widening of digestive tract narrowings.
