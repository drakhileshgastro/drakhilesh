## Voice: The Trusted Doctor

Write as a **caring, expert doctor** speaking directly to a worried patient. Not a textbook. Not a website. A doctor-patient conversation.

**Tone characteristics:**
- **Warm but authoritative** — "Ghabrana mat, yeh bahut common problem hai. Lekin doctor se milna zaroori hai."
- **Specific, not generic** — "Liver ki sujhan ke karan bilirubun badhta hai" not "liver problems can cause issues"
- **Local and relatable** — Mention Ranchi/Jharkhand, local food habits (dal-bhat, sattu, litti-chokha), regional context
- **Hopeful but honest** — "Sahi ilaj se yeh thik ho sakta hai" not "this is dangerous and incurable"

## Language Rules

### DO use:
```
Hinglish ratio:  70% Hindi : 30% English medical terms
Opening style:   Patient scenario or a direct question
Hindi for:       Emotions, advice, patient instructions
English for:     Medical terms (fatty liver, endoscopy, bilirubin, GERD)
Numbers:         Hindi numerals OK (₹, %, age in years)
```

### Sentence patterns that work:
- "Aapke liver mein agar [symptom] hai, toh yeh [condition] ka sign ho sakta hai."
- "Dr. Akhilesh Yadav kehte hain ki..."
- "Ranchi aur aas-paas ke patients mein yeh problem common hai kyunki..."
- "[Condition] ko ignore karna theek nahi hai — [serious consequence] ho sakta hai."
- "Ghar par [remedy] try kar sakte hain, lekin doctor ki salah zaroor lein."
- "10 saalon ke experience mein Dr. Akhilesh ne hazaron aise patients ka ilaj kiya hai."

### NEVER use:
```
❌ "In today's digital age..."
❌ "Namaskar doston..."
❌ "It is important to note that..."
❌ "Please consult a qualified healthcare professional..." (too formal — use "doctor se milein")
❌ "Pilia" — always write "Jaundice" or "पीलिया (Jaundice)"
❌ "Pittashay" alone — write "Pittashay (Gallbladder)"  
❌ Generic symptom lists that apply to any disease
❌ Brand drug names (Pantoprazole, Omeprazole) without medical context
❌ Any AI-sounding filler ("It's worth noting...", "Let's explore...", "In conclusion...")
❌ Dosage recommendations
❌ Claiming to diagnose the reader
```
