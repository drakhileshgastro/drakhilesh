# Medical Content Guidelines — YMYL Rules

## What is YMYL?

"Your Money, Your Life" — Google's highest-scrutiny content category. Medical advice on this site affects patient health decisions. Google penalizes low-quality YMYL content heavily.

**This site is YMYL.** Every blog post is reviewed by Google's quality raters for medical accuracy, trustworthiness, and expert authorship.

---

## ABSOLUTE RULES (Non-Negotiable)

### 1. NO Dosage Advice
❌ "Omeprazole 20mg lein raat ko"
❌ "2 tablet subah khali pet lein"
✅ "Doctor ke prescription ke hisaab se medicine lein"
✅ "Dawai ke bare mein apne gastroenterologist se poochein"

### 2. NO Diagnostic Claims
❌ "Agar aapko yeh symptoms hain, toh aapko fatty liver hai"
❌ "Is test se confirm ho jayega ki aapko cirrhosis hai"
✅ "Yeh symptoms fatty liver ke sign ho sakte hain — doctor se test karwayein"
✅ "Diagnosis ke liye ultrasound aur blood tests ki zaroorat hoti hai"

### 3. NO Treatment Guarantees
❌ "Is diet se fatty liver zaroor thik ho jayegi"
❌ "Yeh nuskha 100% kaam karta hai"
✅ "Sahi ilaj aur lifestyle changes se fatty liver improve ho sakti hai"
✅ "Bahut se patients mein significant improvement dekhi gayi hai"

### 4. NO Emergency Minimization
❌ "Khoon ki ulti ghabrane wali baat nahi hai, ghar par rest karein"
❌ "Aankhon mein pilappan aam baat hai, kuch dino mein thik ho jayega"
✅ "Khoon ki ulti ek medical emergency hai — IMMEDIATELY doctor ke paas jayein"
✅ "Aankhon mein pilappan (jaundice) liver disease ka sign ho sakta hai — 24 ghante mein doctor se milein"

### 5. NO Brand Drug Names Without Context
❌ "Pantoprazole lein" (prescription drug mentioned casually)
❌ "Rifaximin se treatment hota hai" (without explaining it's prescription-only)
✅ "Doctor prescribed medicines se treatment hota hai"
✅ "Antacids (jo doctor likhte hain) se temporary relief milta hai"

### 6. NO Unverified Statistics
❌ "90% patients fatty liver se completely recover kar lete hain" (unverified)
❌ "Yeh condition sirf 5% logon mein serious hoti hai" (unverified)
✅ Use only statistics from: AIIMS, ICMR, WHO, published medical journals
✅ If unsure: remove the statistic or write "research ke anusaar" without specific numbers

### 7. NO Traditional Medicine Claims Without Qualification
❌ "Haldi se liver thik ho jata hai"
❌ "Neem ki patti se jaundice ka ilaj hota hai"
✅ "Kuch ghar ke nuskhe temporary relief dete hain, lekin yeh doctor ki medicine ki jagah nahi le sakte"
✅ "Haldi mein curcumin hota hai jo studies mein liver protective dikhaya gaya hai — lekin doctor se poochein pehle"

## Content That Needs Extra Care

### Liver Disease Content
- Do NOT imply liver cirrhosis is always reversible (it is not — fibrosis can be irreversible)
- Do NOT minimize hepatitis B — it is a chronic, potentially serious infection
- Always mention: "Liver biopsy, MRI ya Fibroscan se disease ki severity pata chalti hai"

### Gallstone Content
- Do NOT say gallstones can be "dissolved" by home remedies (unproven)
- Do NOT discourage surgery if doctor has recommended it
- Always: "Surgery ki zaroorat hai ya nahi, yeh doctor decide karte hain"

### Endoscopy/Procedure Content
- Do NOT promise "completely painless" — say "bahut kam dard hota hai, anesthesia diya jata hai"
- Do NOT give pre-procedure diet advice without standard protocol context
- Always: "Procedure se pehle doctor aaapko complete instructions denge"

### Cancer-Related Content
- NEVER make predictions about prognosis
- NEVER say "yeh zaroor cancer nahi hai"
- Always recommend early screening: "Early detection se treatment zyada effective hota hai"
- Use: "malignancy ke liye biopsy ki jaati hai"
