# Medical Content Guidelines — drakhileshgastro.com
## NMC + ASCI Compliance for YMYL Medical Content

This document governs ALL medical claims on drakhileshgastro.com. Every writer and reviewer must know these rules. Violations expose the practice to NMC disciplinary action and ASCI complaints.

---

## 1. Governing Authorities

### NMC (National Medical Commission)
The NMC Code of Medical Ethics 2023 governs doctor advertising in India:
- Doctors may share educational health content
- Doctors may NOT make comparative claims ("best doctor in India")
- Doctors may NOT guarantee outcomes ("100% cure rate")
- Doctors may NOT use patient testimonials that imply guaranteed results

### ASCI (Advertising Standards Council of India)
ASCI Chapter I.3 — Healthcare:
- No advertisements shall contain claims that a product/service can cure any condition
- Superlative claims require substantiation
- "Best", "fastest", "most effective" — require verifiable evidence

## 2. Hard Prohibitions — NEVER Publish

### Category A — Outcome Claims (NMC + ASCI violation)
```
FORBIDDEN:
"Yeh treatment aapko 100% theek kar degi"
"Permanent cure for fatty liver"
"Guaranteed results"
"We have cured 4,000+ patients" (implies all cured)

ALLOWED:
"Dr. Akhilesh Yadav ne 4,000+ patients ki consult ki hai"
"Most patients show improvement with appropriate treatment"
"Early-stage fatty liver can improve significantly with lifestyle changes"
```

### Category B — Diagnostic Claims (Medical Council violation)
```
FORBIDDEN:
"Agar aapko yeh symptoms hain toh aapko fatty liver hai"
"Your bilirubin being high means you have hepatitis"
"This means you have IBS"

ALLOWED:
"Yeh symptoms fatty liver ke common symptoms hain — diagnosis ke liye doctor se milein"
"High bilirubin can indicate various conditions — Dr. Akhilesh Yadav evaluation karenge"
```

### Category C — Dosage Advice (Medical Practice Act violation)
```
FORBIDDEN:
"Take omeprazole 20mg twice daily"
"Liver tonic 2 tablespoons morning and evening"
"Ayurvedic remedy: 5g amla powder in water"

ALLOWED:
"Doctor ke prescription ke anusar antibiotic course pura karein"
"PPI medications — jo aapke doctor ne prescribe ki hain — sahi time par lein"
```

### Category D — Superlative Claims (ASCI violation without proof)
```
FORBIDDEN:
"Best gastroenterologist in Ranchi"
"Top-rated doctor in Jharkhand"
"India's most trusted GI specialist"

ALLOWED:
"Dr. Akhilesh Yadav — DM Gastroenterology specialist in Ranchi"
"4.9/5.0 Google Rating" (verifiable, from Google)
"One of Ranchi's experienced DM Gastroenterologists" (qualified claim)
```

### Category E — Procedure Claims (Absolute safety claims)
```
FORBIDDEN:
"Endoscopy is completely painless"
"100% safe procedure"
"No risk involved"

ALLOWED:
"Endoscopy mein IV sedation diya jaata hai — most patients minimal discomfort report karte hain"
"Modern endoscopy ek well-established safe procedure hai — risks rare hain"
"Dr. Akhilesh Yadav sedation ke saath comfortable experience ensure karte hain"
```

## 3. Required Disclaimers

### Every Blog Post — Mandatory
Include this in the final tip/expert section:
> "Yeh article sirf educational purpose ke liye hai."

### Symptom / Disease Pages — Mandatory
> "Yeh jaankari sirf educational purpose ke liye hai. Kisi bhi symptom ke liye apne doctor se milein — self-diagnosis na karein."

### Diet / Lifestyle Pages — Mandatory
> "Koi bhi diet ya lifestyle change karne se pehle apne doctor se salah zaroor lein."

## 4. Emergency Symptom Protocol

When writing about serious/alarming symptoms, NEVER minimize them. Use this framework:

### Red Flag Symptoms — Must Escalate Urgently
If any of these appear in the blog topic, explicitly tell patients to seek IMMEDIATE care:
- Hematemesis (vomiting blood)
- Melena (black tarry stools)
- Severe abdominal pain with rigid abdomen
- Jaundice + fever + pain (Charcot's Triad — cholangitis)
- Loss of consciousness with liver disease
- Acute severe rectal bleeding

**Required language for emergencies:**
> "Yeh ek emergency condition hai. Turant nearest hospital jaayein ya ambulance call karein."

### Alarm Symptoms — Prompt Care (not emergency but urgent)
- Weight loss >5% unexplained
- Progressive dysphagia
- Blood in stool (non-hemorrhoid cause)
- Persistent jaundice
- New-onset ascites

**Required language for alarm symptoms:**
> "Yeh symptoms mein bina der kiye Dr. Akhilesh Yadav ya kisi gastroenterologist se milein."

## 5. Evidence Standards

### Claims That Are OK Without Citation
Well-established medical consensus — no citation needed:
- "Hepatitis B vaccine prevents hepatitis B infection"
- "Fatty liver se cirrhosis ho sakta hai agar untreated rahe"
- "H. Pylori peptic ulcer ka common cause hai"
- "Colonoscopy colon cancer screening ka gold standard hai"

### Claims That Need Qualification
Specific statistics — use ranges or qualify:
```
WRONG: "Fatty liver 70% Indians mein hota hai"
RIGHT: "Fatty liver estimates vary — India mein prevalence increasing hai, especially urban areas mein"

WRONG: "ERCP 99% success rate hai"
RIGHT: "ERCP mein experienced hands mein success rate kaafi high hai — Dr. Akhilesh Yadav se discuss karein"
```

### Claims to Avoid Entirely
- Specific survival rates for cancer
- Specific cure rates for any condition
- Specific cost guarantees
- Comparative claims vs other doctors or hospitals

## 6. Patient Testimonials — Compliance Rules

### Allowed
- Anonymized or first-name-only patient scenarios used as illustrative examples
- Google Review ratings (verified, from Google)
- General outcome descriptions: "Many patients show improvement"

### Not Allowed
- Specific named patients without written consent
- "Mr. XYZ from Dhanbad was completely cured" (outcome guarantee implication)
- Before/after medical images without ethics approval
- Testimonials that suggest the treatment will work for everyone

### Correct Format for Patient Stories
```
CORRECT:
"Ranchi ke ek 48-saal ke patient — jo fatty liver se pareshan the — ne 6 mahine ki lifestyle change ke baad improvement dekhi."

WRONG:
"Ramesh Kumar, Ranchi: 'Dr. Akhilesh ne mujhe 100% theek kar diya! 6 mahine mein fatty liver bilkul khatam ho gayi!'"
```

## 7. Drug and Treatment Names

### Generic Names — Allowed with Context
```
ALLOWED (educational context):
"Antiviral medications jaise tenofovir aur entecavir Hepatitis B ke liye use hote hain — doctor ke prescription ke anusar."

NOT ALLOWED:
"Tenofovir 300mg ek baar roz leni chahiye."
```

### Traditional / Ayurvedic Claims
Avoid making therapeutic claims about ayurvedic or home remedies:
```
ALLOWED: "Haldi mein curcumin hota hai jiske anti-inflammatory properties par research ho rahi hai."
NOT ALLOWED: "Haldi se liver ki saari bimariyan theek ho jaati hain."
```

---

## 8. Competitive Claims

### About Other Doctors / Hospitals — Never Mention
- Do not name competing doctors or hospitals
- Do not compare costs/outcomes with other providers
- Do not say "better than" any named alternative

### About Ranchi vs Other Cities
This comparison is allowed (factual):
```
ALLOWED:
"Pehle patients ERCP ke liye Kolkata ya Patna jaate the — ab Ranchi mein Dr. Akhilesh Yadav ke paas available hai."

"Jharkhand ke patients ko ab advanced GI care ke liye bahar nahi jaana padta."
```

## 9. Quick Reference — Allowed vs Not Allowed

| Claim Type | Allowed | Not Allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Doctor credentials | "DM Gastroenterology from [university]" | "India's best gastroenterologist" |
| Patient count | "4,000+ consultations" | "4,000+ patients cured" |
| Procedure safety | "Modern, well-established procedure" | "100% safe, no risk" |
| Treatment outcomes | "Most patients improve with treatment" | "Guaranteed cure" |
| Pain claims | "Sedation diya jaata hai — minimal discomfort" | "Completely painless" |
| Cost | "Competitive pricing — call for details" | "Cheapest in Jharkhand" |
| Comparison | "Ranchi mein DM Gastroenterologist available" | "Better than Patna/Kolkata" |
| Ratings | "4.9/5 Google Rating" (with basis) | "Top-rated in India" (no basis) |
